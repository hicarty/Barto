How it works

From brief to verified result

Every engagement follows the same three steps. We agree the target and budget, run the acquisition, and hand over results you can check.

01

Brief & budget

We agree the product or cause, the audience, the targets and the budget, whether that's a sales commission model or a pre-allocated fundraising budget.

02

Acquire

Outbound sales for commercial partners and face-to-face ambassador campaigns for fundraising partners, each run to its own standards.

03

Verify & hand over

Qualified leads go to your sales team to close, and fundraising sign-ups are verified before they count.

The handover model

We find and qualify, and you close. Partners keep the customer relationship and pay for results, not for headcount.

Compliance first

Fundraising follows the Code of Fundraising Practice, the Charities Acts and UK GDPR. Vulnerable people are protected, and no one is pressured.

Independent & cross-sector

Integrate is an independent business. Running media, software and fundraising work side by side lets us share what works across sectors.
